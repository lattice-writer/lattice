# Boards

A board is a whiteboard saved in your project as an [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) `.excalidraw` file, drawn natively by Lattice. Use it for figures you are still thinking through, diagrams to show co-authors, or notes beside the paper. Boards from excalidraw.com open as they are, and boards you make here open there.

## Create a board

In the **Project** panel's **+** menu, choose **New board**: it creates `untitled.excalidraw` and opens it. You can also right-click a folder or an empty area, choose **New file** and give the name an `.excalidraw` ending, for example `ideas.excalidraw`. An existing `.excalidraw` file from elsewhere comes in through **Import files…** in the same menu.

## Draw

The toolbar at the top has the lock (keeps the current tool selected), the hand (pans the canvas), selection, rectangle, diamond, ellipse, arrow, line, draw (a freehand pen), text and eraser. **More tools** holds image, frame and lasso selection. Arrows snap to shapes and stay attached when you move them.

- **Bottom left:** zoom out, the zoom level, zoom in, then undo and redo.
- **Bottom right:** **Help**, with the keyboard shortcuts.
- **Top left:** the menu, with **Find on canvas** (`⌘F`), **Export image...** (`⌘⇧E`), **Help** and resetting the canvas. Export writes a PNG or SVG of the board or the selection, with or without the background, in dark mode, at a scale you choose, or copies it to the clipboard.

![The tutorial's attention-map.excalidraw board, with labelled boxes and arrows, beside the PDF](images/en-board.webp)

Boards save as you draw, appear in [History](history.md) like any other file, and sync to Overleaf as ordinary files. The Agent can read and edit them too.
